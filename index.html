How to play

The deal

The first five cards deal themselves — one to each of the four hands, one face down to the crib. You place every card after that into a hand of your choice. Never the crib; once placed, a card stays.

The crib feeds itself

No hand may hold more than one card beyond the crib. Each time all four hands catch up, the next card deals itself face down into the crib. You only see it at the count.

The count

When all twenty cards are out, a starter flips and counts in every row. Fifteens score 2, pairs 2, runs their length, four of a suit a flush (the crib needs all five), a jack matching the starter suit is a nob.

Par is 29

Total all five rows, subtract 29. Over par pegs you up the rail; under pegs you backwards — totals can go negative.

Winning

Everyone plays the same number of rounds. The match ends once rounds are equal and someone has 29 or more — if a rival gets there first, you still get your answer round. Highest total wins; equal totals tie.

Online tables

2–4 players, turn-based in seat order, each with their own fresh shuffle. Empty seats can be filled with AI (played by the host's device — the table waits if the host is offline). Public tables are listed for anyone; private rooms use a five-letter code. While you wait, you watch the active player place every card live.

Dropped connection?

Your seat is held. Rejoin with the same name and room code — or tap your table in the lobby — and your half-played round comes back exactly as you left it.

Tips

Fives are gold — every face card makes fifteen with them. Keep one hand loose early as a dumping ground, and remember the hidden crib counts too.
