Chinese Cribbage
FIVE HANDS · ONE STARTER · PAR 29

Twenty cards.
Place them well.

Place your cards into four hands while the crib deals itself face down, flip the starter, and count every row like a cribbage hand. Beat par and you peg up the rail. Miss it and you peg backwards. First past 29 — after equal rounds — takes the match.

The deal

The first five cards deal themselves — one to each hand and one, face down, to the crib. After that, you place each drawn card into a hand of your choice. Never the crib, and once a card is placed, it stays.

The crib feeds itself

No hand may hold more than one card beyond the crib. Each time all four hands catch up, the next card deals itself, face down, into the crib — and you only see it when it's time to count.

The starter

When the rows are full, a starter is flipped. It counts as the fifth card in every row, just as in regular cribbage.

Scoring

Fifteens score 2, pairs 2, runs their length, four of a suit a flush (the crib needs all five), and a jack matching the starter suit is a nob.

Par is 29

Total all five rows, subtract 29. Over par pegs you up; under par pegs you down — totals can go negative.

Winning

The match ends only when both players have played equal rounds and someone has 29 or more. If your opponent gets there first, you still get your answer round. Highest total wins; equal totals are a tie.

Tips

Fives are gold — every face card pairs with them for fifteen. Keep one row loose early as a dumping ground, and remember the crib scores too.
