Chinese Cribbage — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Chinese Cribbage website and the Android and iOS apps. Last updated November 2026.

The short version

Offline play (solo, versus AI, pass & play) stores nothing about you. If you use online play, we store the game data needed to run tables, Conquest, friends and leaderboards. We don't show ads or use analytics, we don't sell or share your data, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we store and why

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or payment details. Coins and Premium are earned by playing; there are no real-money purchases.

Where it's stored

Online data is stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication), operated by Google. Sign-in is handled by Google or Apple; we never see your password. See Firebase's privacy information.

Who can see it

Other players can see your display name, level, banner, Conquest stars, game stats and online status. Your email is never displayed to other players. The game's administrator can view and adjust account game data (for example coins, hearts and Premium) to fix problems.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings (⚙) → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, stats, progress, coins, friends list, friend code and leaderboard entry, and your sign-in record. Moves you already played at shared tables may remain in that table's history, without your account attached.

Guest sessions can be deleted the same way while you're still in them. If you can't use the app, ask for deletion by opening an issue at github.com/StephenBulldogs/ChineseCribbage/issues. Don't include private details in a public issue: we'll follow up with you.

Children

Chinese Cribbage is a general-audience card game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
